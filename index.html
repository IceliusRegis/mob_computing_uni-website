Welcome to Tracen Academy!

Tracen Academy is a prestigious institution that serves as a combined school and training center for "Umamusume," horse-girl-like beings with the ears and tails of horses. Students enroll from middle school through high school to develop their talents in both academic subjects and specialized racing, with the ultimate goal of becoming famous racers and performers in the professional Twinkle Series and other events. The academy offers a comprehensive curriculum, on-site and external training facilities, and provides the infrastructure for Umamusume to mature, compete, and eventually pursue careers in racing or other fields after their education.
